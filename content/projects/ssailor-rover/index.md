Contributed CAD design and prototype development for the SSailOR (Spherical Sailing Omnidirectional Rover) at NC State University. This wind-propelled autonomous platform uses adjustable sails for locomotion and directional control on planetary surfaces — enabling persistent exploration without traditional power systems.

**My contributions:** SolidWorks CAD modeling, iterative prototype geometry for wind tunnel testing, and fabrication of test models. Worked alongside PhD researchers in NCSU's Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering department. Co-author on arXiv publication (2508.12443).
